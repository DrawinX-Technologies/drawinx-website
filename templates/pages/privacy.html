Privacy Notice

Last updated: 3 November 2026

DrawinX respects your privacy. This notice explains what information we may collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Who Operates This Website?

This website is operated by the team using the name DrawinX Technologies. DrawinX Technologies is currently an unregistered business name and is not being represented as a registered company or separate legal entity.

For privacy-related questions or requests, please use the Contact page on this website.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our Contact form, we may collect:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your organisation name, if you choose to provide it.
  • The category of your enquiry.
  • The message you submit.

Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, confidential business information, or other sensitive personal information in the form.

Our website and hosting infrastructure may also process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, request timestamps, and server logs, for website operation, security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. The exact information and retention period may depend on the hosting provider and its configuration.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use enquiry information to:

  • Read and respond to your questions.
  • Discuss service requests and potential collaborations.
  • Follow up on an enquiry where necessary.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the website.
  • Prevent spam, misuse, and fraudulent submissions.

We do not use enquiry information for unrelated purposes.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

We do not currently use website analytics services or advertising trackers.

The website may use strictly necessary technical storage or cookies required for functions such as form security. These are not used by us for advertising or behavioural analytics.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking in the future, we will update this notice and provide any choices or consent mechanisms required by applicable law.

5. How Information Is Stored and Shared

Enquiry information may be stored in the website's database and processed by service providers needed to operate the website, such as hosting, database, or email-delivery providers.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share enquiry information for third-party advertising.

Access to enquiry information should be limited to people who need it to handle enquiries, maintain the website, or protect its security. Service providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We aim to retain enquiry records for no longer than 12 months after the enquiry has been closed. After that, we will delete or anonymise them unless we need to retain particular information for a legitimate legal, security, or dispute-resolution reason.

Technical logs, backups, and records maintained by hosting or other service providers may follow separate retention periods set by those providers.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You can contact us to:

  • Ask what personal information you have submitted to us.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your enquiry information.
  • Raise a privacy concern.

We will review and respond to reasonable requests, subject to applicable legal requirements and any information we are legitimately required to retain.

Use the Contact page to submit a privacy-related request. Please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant enquiry, but do not send passwords or identity documents unless we specifically establish a safe and necessary process.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to encourage children to submit personal information. Please do not knowingly submit a child's personal information through the Contact form unless the required parent or guardian consent and other applicable legal requirements have been addressed.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us inappropriately, contact us through the Contact page so we can review the matter.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. However, no website, database, or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please avoid sending sensitive or confidential information through ordinary website forms or email.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. Those websites operate under their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for their content, security, or privacy policies, so please review their notices before sharing information with them.

11. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice when our website, services, or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

12. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Notice or your personal information, please use the Contact page on the DrawinX website and identify your message as a privacy-related enquiry.

This notice describes our stated current practices. It should be updated whenever our data collection, storage, service providers, or other privacy practices change.
